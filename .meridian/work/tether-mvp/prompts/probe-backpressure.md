# P5 probe: a phone that stops reading (backpressure on spotty links)

tether's promise is "usable on spotty, barely-there connections" and "everything bounded" (`docs/design/plan.md` principles and §Resource budget; `docs/design/daemon-core.md` §6 limits: per-client queues, what happens when a socket can't drain — resync/snapshot, pause, close). The budget probe (p155, `.meridian/work/tether-mvp/evidence/budget/README.md`) only measured clients that kept reading. Establish on the running daemon what happens when a watching client stops reading while its pane floods (`yes`, and 1 MiB/s mixed escapes): daemon RSS over time (must plateau at the documented bound), what tmux sees (is the pane paused via `refresh-client -A pause`/flow control, does the laptop's attached client stay responsive), what the client gets when it resumes reading after 10 s, 60 s, 5 min (a fresh snapshot? the right content?), and whether a second healthy client on another session is unaffected. Also a client whose TCP stalls mid-frame (SIGSTOP the client process) vs one that reads slowly (~10 KiB/s).
Run on this Mac, natively (no Docker; the user asked to keep CPU down), the daemon the way `web/e2e/shared/stack.ts` starts it, against isolated `-L` tmux 3.4 and 3.7b from /tmp/tether-meridian-501/tmux/. Write a throwaway WebSocket client under /tmp/tether-meridian-501/bp-<spawn id>/ (Node is available; pnpm, never npm) that speaks the protocol (`src/proto/`, generated TS under `web/`). No source edits; if you find a defect give file:line and a proposed fix. Record numbers and commands in `$MERIDIAN_ACTIVE_WORK_DIR/evidence/backpressure/README.md` (excerpts). Clean up processes and tmux servers you start. Report: per scenario expected vs observed, and defects.
Spawn env TMPDIR=/tmp/tether-meridian-501.
