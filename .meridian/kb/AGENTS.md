# KB Guide

Cross-cutting knowledge for tether that spans components and outlives sessions:
settled decisions, where canonical design lives, and the gates that still stand
between design and shipped behavior. Pages record the project's current settled
intent, independent of any checkout.

The canonical design lives in the tether repository under `docs/design/`. KB pages
point into it and record what a cold agent must know to use it correctly; they do
not restate specs. Subsystem detail stays with its subsystem.

Raw research and transcripts don't belong here; synthesize them first. Active task
plans live in work directories. When content is superseded, delete it or move it to
`archive/`. Pages read as current truth, never narrate their own evolution.

- `index.md`: catalog. Update when you create or modify a page.
- `.context/CONTEXT.md`: writing conventions, structure, validation.
